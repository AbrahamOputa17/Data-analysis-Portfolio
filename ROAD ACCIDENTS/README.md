# ROAD ACCIDENTS

## This project presents a Power BI Road Accident Analysis Dashboard designed to identify accident trends, severity, casualties, and major risk factors. It transforms raw accident records into actionable insights that can support road authorities, policymakers, and safety organizations.

## The main objective is to understand when, where, and under what conditions accidents occur and identify factors linked to serious or fatal outcomes. Key KPIs include total accidents, total casualties, fatal and serious accidents, accident severity rate, accident trends, and accidents by location, vehicle type, weather, road type, and road conditions.

## Potential datasets include UK Road Safety datasets, Kaggle accident datasets, government crash records, or Nigerian FRSC data. Useful fields include accident date, location, severity, casualties, vehicle type, weather, road surface, road type, speed limit, light conditions, and coordinates.

## Key questions include: When do accidents peak? Which locations are most dangerous? What factors contribute to severe accidents? How do weather and road conditions affect accident rates? Which vehicle types are most involved? Which areas require safety interventions?

## The workflow involves collecting data, cleaning missing and duplicate records, standardizing categories, transforming dates, creating calculated measures, exploring patterns, modelling the data, developing KPIs, building interactive dashboards, identifying insights, and making recommendations.

## Recommended visuals include KPI cards, trend charts, severity breakdowns, bar charts, maps, condition-based comparisons, slicers, and drill-through pages.

## Recommendations include improving infrastructure and lighting in high-risk areas, strengthening enforcement during peak periods, improving road maintenance, and targeting safety campaigns toward conditions associated with severe accidents.
