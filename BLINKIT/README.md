# Financial Performance Analysis

## This project involved performing an end-to-end business intelligence analysis using Power BI. The objective was to transform raw sales data into actionable insights that help stakeholders monitor business performance, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions.

## The workflow began with data collection and cleaning, where missing values, inconsistencies, and formatting issues were addressed using Power Query. After preparing the dataset, data modeling techniques were applied to establish relationships between tables and create a structured analytical model.

## Using DAX (Data Analysis Expressions), several key performance indicators (KPIs) were developed, including total sales, profit, revenue growth, customer performance, and product-level metrics. Interactive visualizations such as bar charts, line charts, KPI cards, slicers, and trend analyses were then designed to provide a comprehensive overview of business performance.

## The final dashboard enables users to:
## Monitor sales and profit trends over time.
## Identify top-performing products and categories.
## Analyze regional and customer-level performance.
## Track business KPIs through interactive filters and visualizations.
## Support strategic decision-making through data-driven insights.
