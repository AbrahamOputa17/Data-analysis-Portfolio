# ECOMMERCE SALES

## This project analyzes e-commerce sales data using Power BI to understand revenue, profitability, product performance, customer behavior and regional trends. The goal is to help businesses identify growth opportunities, improve profitability and make data-driven decisions. Since the video provides limited context, reasonable assumptions are applied.

## The business problem is to determine what drives sales and profit, identify top and underperforming products, evaluate regional performance and understand how discounts affect profitability.

## Key insights focus on sales and profit trends, high-performing categories, valuable customers, profitable regions and products with weak margins. A suitable dataset should contain Order ID, Order Date, Customer, Region, Category, Product, Quantity, Sales, Discount, Cost and Profit. A Superstore-style dataset can recreate the analysis.

## KPIs include Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, Orders, Quantity Sold, Average Order Value, Customers and Growth Rate.

## Analytical questions include: Which products and categories generate the most profit? Which regions perform best? How do sales change over time? Which customers contribute the most revenue? Are discounts reducing profit?

## The workflow involves collecting data, cleaning missing values and duplicates, correcting data types, creating relationships and DAX measures, exploring trends, identifying patterns, building dashboards, generating insights and providing recommendations.

## Recommended visuals include KPI cards, sales/profit trend charts, category and product rankings, regional maps, sales-versus-profit charts, discount analysis and interactive slicers.

## Recommendations include focusing on high-margin products and regions, reviewing underperforming products, controlling excessive discounts, targeting valuable customers and continuously monitoring KPIs.
