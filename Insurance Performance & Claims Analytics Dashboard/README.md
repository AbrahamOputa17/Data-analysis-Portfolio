# Insurance Performance & Claims Analytics Dashboard

## This project focuses on building an interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing insurance business performance. The objective is to transform raw insurance data into actionable insights about policies, customers, premiums, and claims, helping management monitor performance and identify areas of risk and opportunity.

## The analysis examines key metrics such as total policies, active policies, premium revenue, claim amounts, average claim value, claim settlement rate, and loss ratio. It can also evaluate performance by policy type, customer demographics, geography, and time period.

## Potential datasets include customer, policy, premium, claims, agent, coverage, geographic, and calendar data. The workflow involves collecting the data, cleaning and transforming it with Power Query, handling missing values and duplicates, creating relationships through a structured data model, performing exploratory analysis, developing DAX measures, and designing an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Recommended dashboard pages include an executive overview, customer and policy analysis, claims and risk analysis, and performance trends. Suggested visualizations include KPI cards, line charts, bar charts, stacked charts, maps, tables, slicers, drill-throughs, and interactive tooltips.

## Key questions include: Which policies generate the most premium? Which customer segments have the highest claims? Which regions perform best? What percentage of claims are settled or rejected? Which products have the highest loss ratios? How do premiums and claims change over time?

## Potential recommendations include focusing on high-value customer segments, reviewing high-loss policy types, improving claims processing, monitoring inactive policies, and using geographic and demographic insights to improve marketing and resource allocation.

## Tools: Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Excel/CSV, SQL, and data modeling.
