# SHOPIFY ANALYSIS

## This project demonstrates an end-to-end Shopify e-commerce sales analysis built in Power BI. The dashboard transforms raw sales, customer and product data into interactive insights that help monitor revenue, customer behavior, product performance and the online purchase funnel. Some business details are reasonably inferred because the source dataset is not provided.

## The goal is to improve business decisions by identifying top-selling products, measuring customer value, tracking sales trends and detecting where customers abandon the buying process.

## The analysis highlights revenue growth over time, high-performing product categories, repeat customer behavior, average order value and funnel conversion opportunities. It emphasizes using clean data, DAX measures and visual storytelling to support decisions.

## A similar project can be recreated using Shopify sales exports, Kaggle e-commerce datasets, Online Retail data, or custom Orders, Customers, Products and Funnel tables.

## Key KPIs include Total Revenue, Orders, Customers, Average Order Value, Units Sold, Conversion Rate, Repeat Customer Rate, Retention Rate and Revenue Growth.

## Important business questions include: Which products generate the most revenue? Which customer segments are most valuable? How do sales change over time? Where do customers drop off in the purchase funnel? Which categories need improvement?

## The workflow follows data collection, cleaning, transformation, modeling, exploratory analysis, dashboard creation, insight generation and business recommendations.

## Recommended visuals include KPI cards, line charts for sales trends, bar charts for product rankings, a customer funnel, customer segmentation, geographic sales maps and interactive slicers.

## Tools used: Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Excel/CSV and SQL. The final dashboard provides actionable insights that improve sales performance, customer retention and overall e-commerce strategy.
