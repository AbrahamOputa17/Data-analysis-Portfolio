# AMAZON SALES ANALYSIS

## This project analyzes sales performance in Power BI to help management understand revenue, profit, products, customers, and regional performance. The dashboard demonstrates data cleaning, modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive visualization.

## The objectives are to monitor sales and profitability, identify top products and customers, compare regions, categories and segments, uncover trends, and support data-driven decisions.

## The analysis can be recreated using a Superstore-style dataset containing orders, products, customers, sales, profit, dates, categories, segments, regions, and states. This is an inferred dataset assumption.

## The workflow includes data collection, cleaning in Power Query, data modeling, DAX measure creation, exploratory analysis, dashboard development, insight generation, and recommendations.

## Key KPIs include Total Sales, Total Profit, Quantity Sold, Profit Margin %, Average Order Value, Sales Growth, and Profit Growth. Important questions include which products and customers generate the most profit, which regions underperform, how categories and segments compare, and where sales are growing but profitability is declining.

## Recommended visuals include KPI cards, sales/profit trend charts, product and category bar charts, regional maps, customer rankings, category/segment comparisons, and slicers for Year, Category, and Segment.

## Business recommendations include investing in profitable products and regions, investigating low-margin products, strengthening high-value customer relationships, improving underperforming categories, and continuously monitoring performance trends.

Tools: Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Excel/CSV.
